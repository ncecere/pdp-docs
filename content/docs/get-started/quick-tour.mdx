This tour follows a team owner at Example Corp, whose Pi Durable Platform runs at `https://agents.example.com`. The names in the screenshots are made up.

## Sign in

Open the platform and choose **Sign in with single sign-on**. It sends you to your company's identity provider. Your administrators may limit sign-in to some groups of people; if you're refused, ask them.

<Screenshot slot="sign-in" />

## Home

**Home** has an **Agents** card, with links to **All agents** and the **Catalog**, and lists **Your teams** with your role in each. Platform admins and auditors also see a **Platform administration** card that opens the admin pages. If you aren't on a team yet, Home says so and, when your administrators set one up, links to where you request a team.

<Screenshot slot="home" />

The sidebar has three parts:

- **The workspace**: **Home**, **All agents** (every agent of every team you're on) and **Catalog** (the templates every team can deploy).
- **The team switcher**: choose **Personal** (your [personal workspace](/docs/use/personal-workspace)) or one of **Your teams**. The team's pages follow: **Overview**, **Agents**, **Templates**, **Models**, **MCP servers**, **Usage**, **Service accounts** and **Team settings**. You see the pages your role allows.
- **Your name** at the bottom opens your account menu: **Profile**, **API keys**, **Connected apps**, the colour theme, help (if your administrators set a link) and **Sign out**.

## Deploy an agent

<Steps>
<Step>

Open **Catalog** and pick a template. Its **Overview** shows the instructions of its current version; **Versions** lists every version.

<Screenshot slot="catalog" />

</Step>
<Step>

Choose **Deploy agent**. Pick the team, give the agent a name that is unique in the team, and optionally choose another model the team may use. Deploying needs the editor role or above.

<Screenshot slot="deploy-dialog" />

</Step>
<Step>

The agent opens on its **Test** tab. Send a message. The first one wakes the agent, which takes a few seconds; after that, replies stream in.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Test it

The **Test** tab is a chat, the same conversation an app would have. Conversations are durable: they survive reloads, restarts and hibernation. **New chat** starts another thread. On a team's agent you choose whether a new chat is **Shared** or **Private**. See [Testing an agent](/docs/use/test-chat).

## Use it from an app

The agent's **Overview** tab has a **Use it from your app** card with the requests an app sends: one agent per user of the app, send a message, follow the conversation. An app calls them with a [service account](/docs/teams/service-accounts) key, or as a person who connected it with [OAuth Connect](/docs/build/oauth-connect). Start with [Build apps](/docs/build).

## Watch what it costs

**Usage** shows the team's model requests and tokens per day, model or agent, with an estimated cost when platform admins have set the models' prices. See [Usage](/docs/teams/usage).
