A template is what a Pi Durable Platform agent is deployed from. A team's own templates are on its **Templates** page; the templates every team can deploy are in the **Catalog**.

<Screenshot slot="team-templates" />

## What a template holds

A template has a name, a slug and a description, and a list of **versions**. Each version holds:

- **Instructions**: the agent's system prompt (up to 20,000 characters).
- **The model it runs on**: one of the models the team may use, or the platform's default model. A team can still pick another model for one agent.
- **MCP servers its agents can call**: some servers, each with all its tools or some of them.
- **Apps can give its agents their own MCP servers for a thread**: whether an app may add its user's MCP servers to a thread (see [Thread MCP servers](/docs/build/thread-mcp-servers)).
- **Kind** (since v0.4): what its agents do: **Assistant** (the default), **Triage** or **Deep researcher**, with the kind's settings. See [Triage and deep researcher agents](/docs/teams/agent-kinds).
- **Ask a person before these tools run** (since v0.4): tools that wait for a person's approval, who else may decide, and when an undecided call is denied. See [Tool approvals](/docs/teams/tool-approvals).
- **Let its agents schedule their own reminders and recurring work** (since v0.4, off by default): gives agents a schedule tool. What they set is listed under the agent's **Triggers**. See [Agent-made schedules](/docs/teams/triggers#agent-made-schedules).
- **What changed**: notes about the version.

Agents also get the built-in `get_time` tool. Templates don't carry code: an agent runs the runtime the operator deployed (the platform's own, or a [custom agent](/docs/build/custom-agents)) with these settings.

## Create a template

Editors, admins and owners choose **New template** on the team's **Templates** page. Give it a name and a slug (unique in the team; it identifies the template in the API), and its first version. A new team template is **private**: only the team sees and deploys it.

## Add a version

On the template's **Versions** tab, choose **New version**. Versions never change once made. New agents get the newest version; existing agents keep theirs until someone upgrades them on the agent's page. Through the API, a setting a new version leaves out keeps the current version's.

## Publish it to the catalog

Owners and admins can offer a team template to every team:

<Steps>
<Step>

Choose **Submit to the catalog** on the template's page. It's **Waiting for review**.

</Step>
<Step>

A platform admin approves it or rejects it with a note. Approved, it's in the catalog and every team can deploy it. Rejected, it's private again and the page shows **The request to publish was rejected** with the reviewer's note.

</Step>
</Steps>

**Withdraw the request** cancels a pending request; **Take out of the catalog** makes a published template private again. Agents other teams already deployed from it keep working.

<Screenshot slot="template-submit" />

A template whose current version runs on one of the **team's own models** or attaches one of the **team's own MCP servers** can't go to the catalog: other teams couldn't use them. Move it to platform models and servers first.

While a template is in the catalog, new versions are what every team gets on its next deployment. A version that would use the team's own models or servers is refused while the template is in the catalog or waiting for review.

## Archive a template

Turn on **Archived** in the template's **Settings**. An archived template takes no new deployments. Agents deployed from it keep their version and keep working. You can unarchive it.

## Platform templates

Platform admins write **platform templates**, which are always in the catalog and run on platform models and servers only. Teams can't change them. Without a template, agents use the platform's built-in `assistant` template.
