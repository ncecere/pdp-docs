title: Running a team
description: What a team owns in Pi Durable Platform, the team's pages in the console, and who does what.
---

A team in Pi Durable Platform owns its **agents**, its **templates**, its **service accounts**, and optionally **model providers** and **MCP servers** of its own. Its members test the agents in the console; its apps reach them through the API.

Platform admins create teams and name the first owner. If your platform set up a request form, Home links to it as **Request a new team**.

## The team's pages

Choose the team in the team switcher. The sidebar then lists the team's pages; you see the ones your role allows.

| Page | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Your role, the team's owners, admins and editors, recent changes, and its limits |
| **Agents** | The team's agents: deploy, test, change, upgrade, delete ([Deploying agents](/docs/teams/deploy-agents)); each agent's approvals, triggers and runs ([Tool approvals](/docs/teams/tool-approvals), [Triggers and runs](/docs/teams/triggers)) |
| **Templates** | The team's own templates and their versions, with their kind ([Triage and deep researcher](/docs/teams/agent-kinds)); asking to publish one ([Templates](/docs/teams/templates)) |
| **Models** | The models the team may use, and providers of its own ([Team models](/docs/teams/models)) |
| **MCP servers** | The MCP servers the team may use, and servers of its own ([Team MCP servers](/docs/teams/mcp-servers)) |
| **Usage** | Model requests, tokens and estimated cost ([Usage](/docs/teams/usage)) |
| **Service accounts** | Identities for the team's apps and their keys ([Service accounts](/docs/teams/service-accounts)) |
| **Team settings** | **Members** and open invites, the **Audit log**, and **General** ([Members and roles](/docs/teams/members-and-roles), [Audit log](/docs/teams/audit-log)) |

## Who does what

- **Members** use the team's agents and see its members, service accounts, models, MCP servers, usage and limits.
- **Editors** also deploy and change agents and write team templates, and read the audit log.
- **Admins** also delete agents, manage members (not owners), service accounts and keys, the team's own providers and MCP servers, and agents' triggers and their limits, decide unattended runs' tool approvals, and ask to publish templates.
- **Owners** also manage other owners.

The full table is in [Members and roles](/docs/teams/members-and-roles).

## Archived teams

Only platform admins rename, archive and unarchive teams. An archived team is shown as **Archived (read-only)**: it takes no new agents or templates, its members and service accounts can't be changed, and its service accounts' keys don't work until it is unarchived.

## Limits

A team may have up to 50 agents made by hand (service-managed agents don't count), 100 service accounts, and 2 active keys per service account. See [Limits](/docs/teams/limits).
