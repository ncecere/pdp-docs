title: Service accounts and keys
description: "Identities a Pi Durable Platform team owns for its apps and scripts: roles, keys, rotation without downtime, disabling and deleting."
---

A service account is an identity a Pi Durable Platform team owns, for apps, services and scripts. Its keys don't depend on anyone staying on the team: the team's owners and admins are responsible for it. Personal workspaces have no service accounts.

Open **Service accounts** in the team's sidebar. Members see the accounts; owners and admins create and manage them and their keys.

<Screenshot slot="service-accounts" />

## Create one

<Steps>
<Step>

**Create service account**. Its name is 1 to 64 lower-case letters, digits and dashes, unique in the team, such as `helpdesk-chat`. The audit log shows it as who acted.

</Step>
<Step>

Choose its role:

- **Member**: uses the team's agents, reads its models, MCP servers, usage and limits. Enough for an app that talks to agents someone deployed.
- **Editor**: also creates and changes agents. An app that gives each of its users an agent (`PUT …/agents/by-key/…`) needs it.

A service account is never an owner or admin, and can't change the team.

</Step>
<Step>

Open it and create its first key on the **Keys** tab.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Keys

**Keys › Create key**. Name it after where it's used, and choose when it expires: 1 to 365 days (90 by default). Keys always expire. The key starts with `pdp_sa_` and is **shown once**; store it in the app's secret store.

<Screenshot slot="service-account-key-created" />

An app sends it as a bearer token:

```sh
curl https://agents.example.com/v1/teams/support/agents \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $PDP_SA_KEY"
```

Keys can only be created in the browser. Only the team's owners and admins (and platform admins and auditors) see an account's keys; nobody sees a key's secret after it's created.

### Rotate a key without downtime

An account can have two active keys at once:

1. Create a new key.
2. Deploy it where the old one is used.
3. Revoke the old key on the **Keys** tab (**Revoke…**). It stops working at the next request.

Revoked and expired keys stay listed for 30 days.

## What a service account can reach

A service account acts in its own team only, with its role, on the agent routes: listing and creating agents, threads, messages and events, and the team's templates, models, MCP servers, usage and limits. It can't reach `GET /v1/me`, `GET /v1/teams` or `GET /v1/teams/{team}`, which are about a person (`403 person_required`), and nothing that needs a browser session.

It sees the team's ordinary agents and its own **service-managed** agents, which only service accounts use. See [Agents by key](/docs/build/agents-by-key).

Each service account has its own rate limit, `REQUESTS_PER_MINUTE` (600 a minute by default).

## Disable or delete

- **Disable service account** (in **Settings**) refuses its keys from the next request. Turn it on again to make them work.
- **Delete service account** revokes every key at once and frees its name. The audit log keeps its entries.

While a team is archived, its service accounts can be viewed but not changed, and their keys don't work.

A team may have up to 100 service accounts.
