A team's **Models** page in Pi Durable Platform has two tabs:

- **Models**: every model the team may use. That is the platform's models published to the team (by platform admins, see [Models](/docs/administer/models)) and the team's own.
- **Providers**: the team's own model providers, if it has any.

<Screenshot slot="team-models" />

Template versions and agents choose from these models. Pickers mark the team's own models.

## Add a provider of the team's own

Owners and admins can add a provider for the team, in the browser (never with a key or an app's token), while platform admins allow it.

<Steps>
<Step>

**Providers › Add provider**. Choose its kind: **OpenAI**, **Anthropic**, **OpenAI-compatible (chat completions)**, **OpenAI Responses-compatible** or **Anthropic-compatible**.

</Step>
<Step>

Enter its base URL (OpenAI and Anthropic have a default), its API key, and optionally extra headers sent with every request (up to 20).

</Step>
<Step>

Open the provider and use **Discover models** to list what it offers, or **Add model manually** with its ID. For each model you can set the context window, the maximum output tokens, whether it accepts images and reasons, and its prices.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot slot="team-provider-add" />

The key and header values are encrypted and never shown again, to anyone. Members and editors see the providers and models, without keys.

## The team's only

A team's own models are available to that team alone. They never reach another team: not through its model list, a template, an agent's model or the model gateway. A personal workspace's own models are for that workspace only.

So a team template that runs on one of the team's models can't be published to the catalog, and platform templates can't use team models.

## Proxy only, through the address guard

Agents reach a team's provider only through the platform's model gateway, which adds the key. The provider's address is the team's choice, so the platform guards it:

- It must be `https`.
- It must be a **public** address, or inside one of the **private networks** platform admins listed in **Admin › Settings**.
- Loopback, link-local and cloud metadata addresses are never allowed.
- The address is checked when you save it (`address_not_allowed`), and resolved and checked again on every connection, so a DNS change can't point it somewhere else later. Redirects aren't followed.

If your provider is on your company network, ask your platform admins to add its network.

## When adding is off

Platform admins can stop teams adding providers (**Teams may add their own model providers** in **Admin › Settings**). Existing providers keep working and can still be changed or deleted. In a personal workspace, adding needs that switch and the personal workspace switch for providers.

`GET /v1/teams/{team}/limits` says whether you may add one now (`canAddProviders`).

## Change, turn off, delete

Open a provider: **Models** and **Settings**. You can replace its key, turn it off, or delete it with its models. A provider or model still used by a template or an agent can't be deleted; move them first. Platform admins see every team's providers and can turn off, change or delete them.

Changes are recorded in the team's audit log.
