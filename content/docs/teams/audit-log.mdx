title: Team audit log

Every change to a Pi Durable Platform team is recorded in its audit log, in the same database transaction as the change itself. The log is append-only: entries are never changed or deleted.

Open **Team settings › Audit log**. Owners, admins and editors can read it; members can't. The team's **Overview** shows the most recent changes.

## What's recorded

- **Members**: added, invited, role changed, removed, left; invites revoked and accepted; owners assigned.
- **Agents**: deployed, changed (name, model, version, turned off), moved to or from another team (in both teams' logs), deleted.
- **Templates**: created, new versions, requests to publish, approved, rejected, taken out of the catalog.
- **Service accounts and keys**: created, changed, deleted, keys created and revoked.
- **The team's own model providers, models and MCP servers**: added, changed, deleted, including changes platform admins make.
- **Platform admins reading a conversation**: each read, with the reason they gave (`thread.admin_read`).

Conversations themselves aren't in the log.

## Reading an entry

Each entry has a label, such as **Deployed agent** or **Added template version** (since v0.1.1 every action has one), and says **who** acted and **how**: a person in the browser, a person with an API key (and its name), a person through a connected app (and the app's name), a service account (and the key's name), or the system. It names the target (with its current name, or its last known name if it was deleted), and **View details** shows the fields before and after the change, and the request ID.

## Filters

- **Area**: an area (Agents, Templates, Team and members, Service accounts, Model providers, Models, MCP servers, Conversations) or, by typing, one exact action.
- **Person**: one person's entries, including their API keys and connected apps.
- **Date** range.

## A platform admin read a conversation

An entry **Read a private conversation** (or **Read a team conversation**, for a shared one) means a platform admin opened one of the team's conversations, or one in your personal workspace. It names the admin, the thread, whether it was private, and the **Reason** they gave. Platform admins can't read a conversation without leaving this entry. See [Reading conversations](/docs/administer/reading-conversations).
