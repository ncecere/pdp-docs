## Users

**Admin › Users** lists everyone who has signed in to Pi Durable Platform, with their platform role, status, last sign-in and how many teams they're on. Search by name or email.

People appear the first time they sign in. You can't create them; you can add someone to a team by email before they sign in, which makes an invite.

Open a person to see their profile and access (platform role, email, first and last sign-in), their teams and role in each, their connected apps and their activity (the changes they made, from the audit log).

<Screenshot slot="admin-user-detail" />

Platform admins can:

- **Change the platform role**: none, platform admin or platform auditor.
- **Suspend…** (in the page's ⋯ menu): the person can't sign in, their sessions end, and their API keys and connected apps stop working at the next request. **Reactivate** undoes it.
- **Disconnect** one of their connected apps.

The last active platform admin can't be demoted or suspended.

### Group access ended

When the platform [re-checks identity provider groups](/docs/operate/oidc#re-checking-groups) and finds that someone is no longer in an allowed group, their **Status** reads **Group access ended** instead of **Active**, in the list and on their page. Point at the badge to see why: they are no longer in a group allowed to sign in, the provider no longer vouches for them (disabled, or their grant was revoked or expired), or it couldn't confirm their groups within the grace period.

The person's page repeats the reason and says that their sessions and connected apps were ended. Their API keys stop working while the badge shows, and their teams, agents and data stay. They can sign in again once the provider lists them in an allowed group, and the badge goes. This is not a suspension: **Suspend…** and **Reactivate** are yours alone, and the two never undo each other. There is nothing to do on this page to restore access: change the person's groups at your provider.

The audit log records both ends: **Ended access: no longer in an allowed group** and **Restored access: back in an allowed group**.

## Teams

**Admin › Teams** lists every team and personal workspace, with its kind, owners and status. Filter by kind to see only teams or only personal workspaces.

<Screenshot slot="admin-teams" />

### Create a team

**Create team**: a name, a slug (lower-case, used in URLs and the API; it can't change later), a description, and the **first owner's email**. If that person hasn't signed in yet, they get an invite and become the owner at their first sign-in.

<Screenshot slot="admin-create-team" />

### Manage a team

Open a team: **Overview**, **Members**, **Agents** and **Settings**.

- **Agents** (since v0.1.1) lists the team's agents, service-managed ones too, with template, who manages them, status and last use. Each opens the agent's page, which platform staff see read-only: **Overview** and **Settings**, and **Conversations** for platform admins (reading one still takes a reason). There's no **Test** chat, and staff can't change or hibernate the agent. See [Administering the platform](/docs/administer#what-platform-roles-never-reach).

- **Assign owner** makes someone an owner: it promotes a member, adds someone, or invites them.
- **Settings** renames the team and changes its description.
- **Archive team…** makes it read-only: no new agents or templates, no member or service account changes, and its service accounts' keys stop working. **Unarchive team** reverses it.

Teams aren't deleted.

## Personal workspaces

A personal workspace's page has **Overview**, **Limits**, **Agents** and **Settings**.

- The **Overview** shows its owner, its agents and running agents against its limits, and **Audit this workspace**: links to its agents' settings and status, its usage and its audit log. Platform staff never see its conversations; a platform admin can read one with a reason (see [Reading conversations](/docs/administer/reading-conversations)).
- **Limits** overrides **Max agents**, **Max running at once** and **Monthly budget** for this workspace. Leave a field empty to use the platform's default. The defaults are in [Settings](/docs/administer/settings#personal-workspaces).
- **Agents** lists its agents, as for a team.
- **Settings** can archive it, making it read-only.

## Service accounts

`GET /v1/admin/service-accounts` lists every team's service accounts for platform admins and auditors. In the console, open a team's **Service accounts** page from its overview.
