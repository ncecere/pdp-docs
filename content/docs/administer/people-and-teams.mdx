## Users

**Admin › Users** lists everyone who has signed in to Pi Durable Platform, with their platform role, status, last sign-in and how many teams they're on. Search by name or email.

People appear the first time they sign in. You can't create them; you can add someone to a team by email before they sign in, which makes an invite.

Open a person to see their profile (from the identity provider, as of their last sign-in, including their groups), their teams and role in each, their connected apps and their recent activity.

<Screenshot slot="admin-user-detail" />

Platform admins can:

- **Change the platform role**: none, platform admin or platform auditor.
- **Suspend…**: the person can't sign in, their sessions end, and their API keys and connected apps stop working at the next request. **Reactivate** undoes it.
- **Disconnect** one of their connected apps.

The last active platform admin can't be demoted or suspended.

## Teams

**Admin › Teams** lists every team and personal workspace, with its kind, owners and status. Filter by kind to see only teams or only personal workspaces.

<Screenshot slot="admin-teams" />

### Create a team

**Create team**: a name, a slug (lower-case, used in URLs and the API; it can't change later), a description, and the **first owner's email**. If that person hasn't signed in yet, they get an invite and become the owner at their first sign-in.

<Screenshot slot="admin-create-team" />

### Manage a team

Open a team: **Overview**, **Members** and **Settings**.

- **Assign owner** makes someone an owner: it promotes a member, adds someone, or invites them.
- **Settings** renames the team and changes its description.
- **Archive team…** makes it read-only: no new agents or templates, no member or service account changes, and its service accounts' keys stop working. **Unarchive team** reverses it.

Teams aren't deleted.

## Personal workspaces

A personal workspace's page has **Overview**, **Limits** and **Settings**.

- The **Overview** shows its owner, its agents and running agents against its limits, and **Audit this workspace**: links to its agents' settings and status, its usage and its audit log. Platform staff never see its conversations; a platform admin can read one with a reason (see [Reading conversations](/docs/administer/reading-conversations)).
- **Limits** overrides **Max agents**, **Max running at once** and **Monthly budget** for this workspace. Leave a field empty to use the platform's default. The defaults are in [Settings](/docs/administer/settings#personal-workspaces).
- **Settings** can archive it, making it read-only.

## Service accounts

`GET /v1/admin/service-accounts` lists every team's service accounts for platform admins and auditors. In the console, open a team's **Service accounts** page from its overview.
