An app such as a chat app can reach your agents **as you**, once you connect it with **OAuth Connect**. Pi Durable Platform then gives the app its own tokens for you. The app never sees your password or your identity provider's tokens.

## Connecting an app

The app sends you to the platform. You sign in as usual, then see a page asking whether to allow the app. It names the app, the site that vouches for it, and what it will be able to do:

- **See your agents and chat with them**
- **Create agents in teams where you can**

Choose **Allow** or **Deny**. Either way you go back to the app.

You're asked once per app: your answer is remembered until you disconnect it. Apps your platform admins marked **trusted** skip this page; you still sign in.

An app that registered itself, instead of publishing a document on its own site, is shown as **Unverified**: its name is its own claim. Allow it only if you know where it comes from.

## The app's logo

If the app published a logo, it shows beside its name on this page, in **Connected apps** and on your page in **Admin › Users**. Your browser doesn't load it from the app's site: the platform fetches it, checks it and serves it itself (since v0.1.2). Your browser only talks to the platform, which keeps the console closed to other sites' images.

- The logo must be on a public `https` address, with no redirects, and at most 256 KiB.
- It must be a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, checked by its bytes. SVG isn't accepted.
- The platform keeps it for an hour, so a new logo can take that long to show.
- If there is no usable logo, you see the app's initials instead.

## What an app can do

A connected app acts as you **without your platform role**: an app you connect as a platform admin has no admin powers. It can:

- list and use the agents of your teams and your personal workspace, and create agents where your role allows;
- read and send messages, follow conversations, and rename or delete threads, as you;
- read your teams, their models, MCP servers, usage and limits.

It can't manage members, keys, service accounts, connected apps or anything in Admin. Every request is checked again against your account: if you're suspended or leave a team, the app loses that access at once. Messages it sends are recorded as yours, "via" the app.

## Your connected apps

Your account menu › **Connected apps** lists the apps you allowed, with when you connected them and when they were last used.

<Screenshot slot="connected-apps" />

**Disconnect…** (after you confirm) stops an app at once: all its tokens stop working and your consent is forgotten. To use it again, connect it again.

## How long a connection lasts

- The app's access tokens last an hour; it refreshes them itself.
- A refresh token lasts 30 days and is replaced every time it's used, so an app you use at least monthly stays connected until you disconnect it.
- If an old refresh token is used again more than 30 seconds after it was replaced, the platform treats it as stolen and ends the connection. You connect again.

## Who else can see them

Platform admins and auditors see your connected apps on your page in **Admin › Users**; platform admins can disconnect them. Nobody can see the tokens: the platform stores only digests.
