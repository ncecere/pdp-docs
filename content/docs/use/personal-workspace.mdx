While platform admins have them turned on, Pi Durable Platform gives everyone who signs in a **personal workspace**: a team of your own, with you as its only member. Choose **Personal** in the team switcher to open it.

<Screenshot slot="personal-workspace" />

## What it holds

- **Agents** you deploy from the **catalog**. A personal workspace has no templates of its own.
- **Models** and **MCP servers** that platform admins offer to personal workspaces. If your administrators allow it, you can also add model providers and MCP servers of your own, for this workspace only (see [Team models](/docs/teams/models) and [Team MCP servers](/docs/teams/mcp-servers); they work the same way).
- **Usage** and **Settings**, with the workspace's audit log.

It has no other members, invites or service accounts.

An app you connect with [OAuth Connect](/docs/use/connected-apps) usually creates your agents here. They are yours: you can rename, change or delete them in the console like any other.

## Limits

The **Overview** shows the workspace's limits and how much of them you use:

- **Max agents**: how many agents it may have (5 by default). Agents that apps created for you count too.
- **Max running at once**: how many may be awake at the same time (2 by default). Waking another is refused until one hibernates.
- **Monthly budget**: recorded, not enforced yet.

Platform admins set the defaults for everyone and can change them for one workspace. When you reach a limit, delete an agent, or wait for one to hibernate (10 idle minutes by default), or ask your administrators.

## Who can see it

Only you use its agents and read its conversations. Platform admins and auditors can **audit** it: they see its agents' settings and status, its usage and its audit log, never conversation titles (they read "Title hidden") or content. They can't use or hibernate your agents. A platform admin can open one conversation only by giving a reason, and that read is recorded in your workspace's audit log, where you see it. See [Reading conversations](/docs/administer/reading-conversations).

## If personal workspaces are turned off

No new personal workspaces are created. Existing ones stay; platform admins can archive one to make it read-only.
