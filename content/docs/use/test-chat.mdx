Each agent in Pi Durable Platform has a **Test** tab: a chat with the agent, sending messages the same way an app would. Open it from the team's **Agents** (or **All agents**), then the agent, then **Test**. Every member of the team can use it; service-managed agents have no Test tab because their conversations belong to an app's users.

<Screenshot slot="agent-test-chat" />

## Chats

The list on the left holds the conversations (threads) you can see, most recently used first. Each shows its title, whether a reply is running, and whether the last reply failed.

- **Search chats**, above the list, finds chats whose title contains what you type. **No chats match.** says when none does.
- A long list loads in pages: **Load more chats** at its end shows the next ones.

- **New chat** starts a thread. Its title is the first line of your first message (up to 60 characters).
- On a team's agent, choose **Shared** or **Private** before you send the first message. **Shared** means everyone who can use the agent sees it; **Private** means only you. The choice is fixed by the first message. See [Shared and private threads](/docs/use/shared-and-private-threads).
- Delete a chat to stop it and remove it from the agent's list.

The first time you open the tab it opens the most recently used chat.

## What a message shows

- **Your messages** show who sent them. On a shared thread that may be another member, or an app acting for someone.
- **The agent's replies** stream in as they are written, with any reasoning and tool calls. Under each finished reply is a line such as `model-name · 1,234 tokens · 3.2s`: the model, the tokens it used, how long it took and, when it didn't end normally, why (for example **Length limit** or **Stopped**).
- **Tool calls** show their input, their output and how long they ran.

Older messages, from before the platform recorded these details, show what they have.

A long conversation opens with its latest messages. **Load earlier messages**, at its top, shows the ones before.

## Steering or stopping a reply

While the agent is replying, the message box says **Enter queues a follow-up; Steer joins the reply in progress**, and the send button becomes a stop button.

- **Enter** sends your message as a **follow-up**: it waits behind the reply in progress, and the agent answers it next.
- **Steer**: type a message and choose **Steer**. It joins the reply in progress. The agent reads it after its current round of tool calls, or with its answer, and carries on with it in mind. Use it to correct or narrow a reply without starting over. Your message appears in the conversation where the agent took it in: after the tool results of the reply it changed.
- **Stop** ends the reply in progress; the conversation keeps what was written so far. A stopped reply isn't an error.

## When the agent is asleep or off

- An agent that has hibernated wakes on your message. The first reply takes a few seconds longer.
- If the agent is **turned off** (in its **Settings**), the Test tab says so and refuses new messages. An editor can turn it on again.
- If the agent's model is no longer available to the team, the agent won't start until an editor chooses another model in **Settings**.

<Callout title="Conversations are durable">
A conversation lives in the agent's own database schema. It survives reloads, platform restarts, the agent hibernating and the runtime being replaced. A reply cut short by a restart resumes when the agent next runs.
</Callout>
